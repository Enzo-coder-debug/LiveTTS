Meet LiveTTS

AT A GLANCE

A generative speech model built around a simple idea: a voice should not be limited to a single language, dialect, or emotion. LiveTTS combines zero-shot voice cloning with expressive control and long-form synthesis, bringing a consistent vocal identity to different ways of speaking.
